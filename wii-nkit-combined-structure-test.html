Wii NKit Combined Structure Test

This single diagnostic combines three checks: partition metadata and file boundaries, candidate boot-header locations, and candidate FST range/signature checks. It reads only small ranges from the ISO in a local Worker; it does not upload or copy the whole ISO.

Choose your Super Mario Galaxy NKit ISO, then run the test.

Important: this does not decrypt partition data or boot the game. Candidate offsets are diagnostic hypotheses; the output will tell us what to inspect next.
